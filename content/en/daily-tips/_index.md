# Daily tips

Most AI failures are not exotic — they come from everyday habits: trusting a confident answer, asking without enough context, or skipping a check before shipping. This topic collects short, practical tips for getting real work done with AI without getting burned.

Posts here are written to stand alone: read one, try it the same day, keep what works.

## Where this fits

This is the "in practice" side of the blog. Start with the [post below](/en/daily-tips/why-ai-hallucinates-and-how-to-handle-it/) — why models invent confident-sounding but wrong answers, and how to guard against it — then harden your workflow with [RAG](/en/rag/), [clear prompting](/en/prompting/), and [evals](/en/evals/).
